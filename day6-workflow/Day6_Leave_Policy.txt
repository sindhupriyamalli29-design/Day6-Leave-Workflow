SAMPLE LEAVE POLICY (made up, for training only)

1. Leave types and balance
Every employee gets 12 days of casual leave (CL) and 8 days of sick leave (SL) per calendar year. Leave is credited on 1 January.

2. Applying for leave
Apply for leave in the HR portal. Casual leave must be applied for at least 2 working days in advance. Sick leave can be applied on the same day, or within 2 working days after returning to work.

3. Half-day leave
Half-day leave is allowed for both casual leave and sick leave.

4. Approval
The reporting manager approves or rejects a leave request within 2 working days. If the manager is on leave, the request goes to the backup approver named in the HR portal.

5. Balance limit and loss of pay
An employee cannot apply for more days than their available balance. Extra days are loss of pay (LOP), and only with HR approval.

6. Cancelling leave
Approved leave can be cancelled by the employee before the leave start date. The manager is notified by email.

7. Carry-over of unused leave
Up to 5 days of unused casual leave can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, or they lapse. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

8. Leave during probation
During the first 3 months (probation), employees can take sick leave only. Casual leave can be used from the fourth month.

9. Leave during the notice period
Casual leave during the notice period needs HR approval. Unused casual leave is not paid out when an employee leaves.

10. Company holidays
There are 10 company holidays each year. The list is published in the HR portal every December. A company holiday that falls inside a leave period is not counted as leave.
